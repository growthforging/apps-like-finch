# Apps like Finch for adults (2027)

Finch is a good app. 768K people gave it an average of 4.9 stars on the App Store. Some adults outgrow the pet and want an app that works on something specific. This page lists four and what each one works on and costs.

| App | Works on | Price as listed | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|

## Glissa, if the habit you want to change is a person

Finch rewards any small goal. Glissa works on 11 named patterns: people pleasing, overthinking, anxious attachment, hyper-independence, not saying what you think and 6 more. A 30-second check-in each day shows which one leads for you, and each week you get one small dare for it. The first go to a stranger or a text. Family comes later. There is no pet, and it is iPhone only.

Free quizzes for each pattern: [glissa.io/quiz](https://glissa.io/quiz).

## Fabulous, if you want routines

Closest to Finch in feel, with a coach in place of the pet. It is strongest on mornings and evenings.

## Habitica, if you want a game

Your to-do list becomes a role-playing game with a party and quests. Free at its core.

## Staying with Finch

If what you want is a gentle daily habit with no particular target, Finch still does that better than anything above.
